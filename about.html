About SkyNest Co-Living Spaces

A place to settle into your next chapter

SkyNest Co-Living Spaces is a fictional co-living accommodation provider in Singapore. We offer furnished accommodation for young professionals, digital nomads and international students who are relocating for work, study or other opportunities.

Our story

⌂Who we are

Moving to a new country or city for work or study often means adjusting to a different routine. Finding suitable accommodation is an important part of that move.

SkyNest Co-Living Spaces brings together furnished private rooms and shared facilities for working, studying and spending time with other residents.

Our accommodation packages offer different arrangements, from private studio apartments to shared apartments and flexible short-term stays, helping residents choose an option that suits their needs.

✦Our purpose

To provide convenient co-living accommodation in Singapore for people relocating for work, study or other opportunities.

We aim to explain our accommodation packages clearly so visitors understand what is included, which facilities are available and how to contact us about their requirements.

Find your arrangement

▤Our accommodation packages

Consider how much privacy you need, which facilities you would like to use and how long you plan to stay. Explore the different packages below.

Premium Stay

A private studio apartment for residents who prefer their own independent living space.

  • Weekly cleaning service
  • Utilities included
  • High-speed internet included
  • Access to all facilities
  • Monthly community events pass

Standard Stay

A private bedroom in a shared apartment with access to communal spaces.

  • Fortnightly cleaning
  • Utilities included
  • Access to co-working spaces
  • Shared kitchen and lounge

Short-Term Stay

Flexible furnished accommodation for residents who need a shorter stay.

  • Flexible booking for 1–4 weeks
  • Furnished room
  • Utilities included
  • Limited access to shared facilities

Custom Packages

Accommodation arrangements for residents and organisations with specific requirements.

  • Corporate bookings
  • Long-term stays
  • Residents with accessibility needs
  • Contact us to discuss requirements

All accommodation is subject to availability. Contact SkyNest to discuss the package that best suits your plans and requirements.
