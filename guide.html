Resident Guide

Find out more about the accommodation packages, get ready for move-in day and learn how to use the shared spaces and facilities at SkyNest Co-Living Spaces.

▤Choosing your accommodation package

SkyNest offers different accommodation packages depending on your preferred room arrangement, length of stay and individual requirements.

  • Premium Stay: A private studio apartment with weekly cleaning, utilities and high-speed internet included, access to all facilities and a monthly community events pass.
  • Standard Stay: A private bedroom in a shared apartment, with fortnightly cleaning, utilities included and access to co-working spaces, a shared kitchen and lounge.
  • Short-Term Stay: Flexible accommodation for 1–4 weeks, including a furnished room and utilities, with limited access to shared facilities.
  • Custom Packages: Available for corporate bookings, long-term stays and residents with accessibility needs. Contact SkyNest to discuss the arrangements you require.

✓Move-in checklist

  • Confirm your accommodation package, arrival date and check-in arrangements.
  • Keep your booking confirmation and photo ID handy for check-in.
  • Check what is included in your chosen package before you pack.
  • Pack your toiletries, chargers, personal belongings and any items you may need for your room.
  • Check your room on arrival and report any existing damage or missing items.
  • Ask about key-card access and the arrangements for using shared facilities.

♡Using shared spaces

  • Keep co-working spaces and study pods tidy after use.
  • Clean up after preparing food in the communal kitchens and dining areas.
  • Respect other residents when using the social lounge and shared areas.
  • Keep corridors, doorways and shared walkways clear.
  • Follow the arrangements provided for key-card access and building security.

⚙Reporting a maintenance issue

  1. Note your room number or the exact location of the problem.
  2. Describe the issue and when you first noticed it.
  3. Report it to SkyNest using the contact details provided by the company.

?Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find quick answers to common questions about staying at SkyNest and choosing an accommodation package.

What accommodation packages does SkyNest offer?

SkyNest offers Premium Stay, Standard Stay, Short-Term Stay and Custom Packages. Each option provides a different room arrangement and level of access to facilities.

What is the difference between Premium Stay and Standard Stay?

Premium Stay provides a private studio apartment with weekly cleaning and access to all facilities. Standard Stay provides a private bedroom in a shared apartment, with fortnightly cleaning and access to shared spaces such as the co-working areas, kitchen and lounge.

Can I book a short-term stay?

Yes. The Short-Term Stay package offers flexible accommodation for 1–4 weeks, subject to availability.

Are utilities and internet included?

Utilities are included in the accommodation packages. High-speed internet is included in the Premium Stay package and is available throughout the building.

What facilities can residents use?

Facilities include co-working and study spaces, shared kitchens and dining areas, laundry rooms, a social lounge, rooftop garden, gym and yoga studio. Access may depend on the package selected.

Can I request a package for specific requirements?

Yes. Custom arrangements may be discussed for corporate bookings, long-term stays and residents with accessibility needs. Contact SkyNest to discuss your requirements.

How can I make an enquiry about accommodation?

Visit the Contact Us page and complete the enquiry form with your preferred package, expected dates and any specific requirements. The form on this practice website is a demonstration and does not send information to a server.
