Everyday living

⌂Designed around your everyday life

From focused work and study time to shared meals and community activities, our facilities support a comfortable daily routine.

Arrive and settle in

Our furnished accommodation helps you get started. Utilities are included in the accommodation packages, with high-speed internet available to support your stay.

Make time to focus

Use the shared co-working spaces and study pods to complete your work, attend to your studies or concentrate on individual tasks.

Connect with others

Meet fellow residents in the social lounge, enjoy the communal areas and take part in weekly community events.

Find your fit

▣A stay that suits your plans

Whether you prefer a private studio, a shared apartment or a shorter stay, explore the available accommodation packages.

  • Premium Stay: A private studio apartment with weekly cleaning, utilities, high-speed internet and access to all facilities.
  • Standard Stay: A private bedroom in a shared apartment, with fortnightly cleaning and access to co-working spaces and shared kitchens.
  • Short-Term Stay: Flexible accommodation for 1–4 weeks, including a furnished room, utilities and limited access to shared facilities.
  • Custom Packages: Arrangements for corporate bookings, long-term stays and residents with accessibility needs.

If you have particular requirements, you can contact SkyNest to discuss a suitable package and the available arrangements.
