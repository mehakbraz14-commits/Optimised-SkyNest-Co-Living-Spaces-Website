Our Facilities

Enjoy a range of facilities designed to support working, studying and everyday living. From co-working spaces and study pods to shared kitchens and social areas, SkyNest provides spaces for residents to use during their stay.

Explore the building

⌂Shared spaces

Illustrative photo of a shared lounge

Social lounge

Relax, meet other residents or spend time with friends in the shared social lounge. Residents can also take part in weekly community events and use the communal areas to connect with others.

Illustrative photo of a study and work area

Co-working and study spaces

Use the shared co-working spaces and study pods to complete work, study or prepare for upcoming tasks. High-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the building.

▣Rooms and everyday facilities

SkyNest offers fully furnished private rooms alongside communal kitchens and dining areas, laundry rooms and shared facilities. Residents can also make use of the rooftop garden, gym and yoga studio. The building has 24/7 security and key-card access to support residents during their stay.
